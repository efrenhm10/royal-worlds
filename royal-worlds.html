👑 ROYAL WORLDS

Dynastic Political Sandbox · England · 1461

Enter the World

A living historical simulation where you play one person inside a much larger political world.

Families have their own ambitions. Courts have factions. Marriages are negotiated, not granted. Estates produce wealth. Foreign countries continue acting even when you are not watching — and you don't have to be the monarch to matter.

🏰 Choose Your House

Your house determines your dynasty, relatives, political network, resources and enemies.

Choose Your Person

Historical people are available alongside historically plausible fictional members of the house. You don't have to play the king or queen — a niece, a cousin, or a minor lord shapes this world too.
